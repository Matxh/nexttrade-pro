Server Rules
01
No Financial Advice
Nothing shared in this server — by members or the AI — is financial advice. This is an educational community only. Do not tell others to buy or sell specific assets. You are responsible for your own trades.
02
Respect Everyone
No harassment, hate speech, discrimination, or personal attacks. Disagreements about charts and analysis are fine — be professional about it. Toxic behaviour results in an immediate ban.
03
No Spam or Self-Promotion
No spamming messages, emojis, or images. Do not promote your own Discord, YouTube, Telegram, or any paid service without explicit permission from a moderator.
04
Post in the Right Channel
Keep discussions relevant to the channel topic. Chart analyses go in #chart-analysis, questions in #help, bugs in #bug-reports. Off-topic posts will be moved or deleted.
05
No Scams or Pump & Dump
Absolutely zero tolerance for scam links, phishing, pump-and-dump schemes, or fake giveaways. This results in a permanent ban and will be reported to Discord Trust & Safety.
06
Use English
Please communicate in English in all public channels so moderators can keep the community safe. We may add language-specific channels as the server grows.
07
No NSFW Content
This is a professional trading community. No NSFW images, links, or discussions anywhere in the server.
08
Follow Discord's Terms
All members must follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times. You must be at least 13 years old to use Discord.
